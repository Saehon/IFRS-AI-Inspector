## Core rule

A GitHub commit can provide a dated technical record, but it **does not create patent rights, reserve patentability, or substitute for filing a patent application**. Public disclosure can affect novelty and patentability depending on jurisdiction and timing.

Potentially patentable implementation details should remain private until explicit IP review and a release decision are complete.

## Pre-publication invention gate

Before releasing a novel accounting-assurance algorithm, standards-to-agent mechanism, digital-twin method, deterministic verification layer, knowledge-graph design, evaluation framework, or related technical contribution:

1. record inventors, conception date, technical problem, solution, alternatives, experiments, diagrams, and source-commit hashes;
2. search prior art and relevant scientific/patent literature;
3. distinguish original contribution from IFRS/IAS/ISA/PCAOB/ISSB/EU AI Act materials and third-party software;
4. mark the invention **PRIVATE — PATENT REVIEW PENDING**;
5. obtain qualified patent/legal review where protection is contemplated;
6. choose patent filing first, private/trade-secret treatment, defensive publication, or public research release;
7. publish enabling technical detail only after that decision.

## Research integrity

Patent strategy must not weaken scientific or professional assurance. Claims remain subject to authoritative-source validation, literature review, reproducibility, falsification, adversarial review, provenance, and human approval.

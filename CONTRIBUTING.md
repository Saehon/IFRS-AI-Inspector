# Contributing to IFRS-AI Inspector

Contributions are welcome when they strengthen scientific validity, reproducibility, safety, documentation, or testability.

## Contribution principles

1. Preserve the boundary between authoritative/professional evidence and model-generated content.
2. Do not add copyrighted or restricted standards text without permission.
3. Document the provenance and license of any dataset, model, software dependency, or external artifact.
4. Add tests or reproducible examples for material code changes where practical.
5. Do not present synthetic results, LLM judgments, or local demonstrations as validated professional conclusions.
6. Keep human review explicit for material accounting or assurance judgments.
7. Report negative findings and known limitations rather than hiding them.

## Pull requests

A useful pull request should explain:

- the problem being addressed;
- the proposed change;
- evidence or rationale;
- tests/reproduction steps;
- data and licensing implications;
- known limitations and risks.

## Research contributions

Empirical additions should identify the research question, data source, sample construction, variable definitions, model/specification, evaluation metrics, robustness checks, and reproducibility artifacts.

By contributing, you confirm that you have the right to submit the material under the repository's applicable licensing terms.

# Changelog

All material changes to **IFRS-AI Inspector** should be summarized here in addition to the Git history.

IFRS-AI Inspector is research software. Repository documentation, architectural terminology, benchmark results, or governance design do not imply IFRS Foundation certification, regulatory approval, audit-firm endorsement, legal compliance, or production readiness.

## [Unreleased] — 2026-09-14

### Added
- Professional repository baseline aligned with the NAAIL Product Repository Standard.
- `ROADMAP.md`, `SECURITY.md`, and `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
- `REPRODUCIBILITY.md` for governed research-run reconstruction.
- `DATA_SOURCES.md` for standards, regulatory, filing, synthetic, and research-data provenance.
- `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` for software/model/standards/publication/trademark boundaries.

### Changed
- README framing strengthened around research maturity, evaluation, human review, and independence.
- EU AI Act language reframed as **governance-aware design considerations**, not an unsupported certification or compliance claim.
- Licensing language clarified to distinguish repository-level grants from third-party standards, datasets, models, and external services.

### Governance
- AI-generated interpretation is not authoritative accounting guidance.
- Deterministic checks should remain separate from model-generated narration where feasible.
- Material conclusions require traceable evidence and qualified human review.
- Standards text and other restricted third-party content must not be redistributed without permission.

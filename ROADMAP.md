# IFRS-AI Inspector Roadmap

## Research maturity path

### Phase 1 — Repository and evidence foundations
- maintain a clear standards/evidence boundary;
- document data provenance and rights;
- keep generative reasoning separate from deterministic calculations;
- require citation, contradiction and provenance checks;
- preserve reproducible research manifests.

### Phase 2 — Benchmarkable prototype
- define frozen accounting and assurance test cases;
- add unit and regression tests for deterministic components;
- benchmark retrieval, authority selection, calculation reproducibility and abstention;
- add temporal and adversarial holdouts;
- document model/version/configuration lineage.

### Phase 3 — Digital-twin evaluation
- implement controlled scenario twins for selected accounting topics;
- compare alternative treatments and evidence paths;
- evaluate multi-agent disagreement and escalation;
- quantify calibration, robustness and reproducibility.

### Phase 4 — Independent validation
- run blind evaluation on previously unseen cases;
- invite independent replication and expert review;
- publish failure cases as well as successful cases;
- separate scientific performance claims from professional-readiness claims.

### Phase 5 — Deployment-readiness research
- security and privacy review;
- model-risk and data-governance review;
- jurisdiction-specific legal/regulatory review;
- qualified professional validation;
- explicit human approval before any real-world use.

## Non-goals

The roadmap does not imply IFRS Foundation approval, regulatory certification, audit-firm endorsement, legal compliance, production readiness, or patentability.
